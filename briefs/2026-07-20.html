CENTCOM Confirms Third Fatality; U.S. Strikes Widen to Coastal and Northern Iranian Cities Over the Weekend
Per Fox News and CNN coverage, CENTCOM confirmed a third U.S. service-member killed in action from the recent Al-Azraq base attack in Jordan and reported unidentified remains found at the site, bringing the total U.S. military death toll to 17 since the conflict began in February. Per Al Jazeera and CNN coverage, U.S. forces conducted an eighth consecutive night of strikes Saturday and launched a ninth wave overnight Sunday into Monday, hitting Tabriz in northwestern Iran, the coastal cities of Chabahar and Konarak on the Gulf of Oman, Bandar Mahshahr and Bandar Imam Khomeini on the Persian Gulf, Jask and Sirik along the Strait of Hormuz, and the port city of Bushehr. Per Al Jazeera coverage citing Iranian authorities, at least 50 people have been killed and more than 500 wounded in U.S. strikes over the past three weeks; U.S. strikes have widened to include civilian infrastructure such as bridges and desalination plants. Per IRGC statements cited by Al Jazeera, two commercial oil tankers were struck and immobilized in the Strait of Hormuz over the weekend after attempting to transit what Iran declared an unsafe route. For the FCI Upstream sleeve, the widening target set — particularly the strikes on Bushehr, Bandar Mahshahr, and Bandar Imam Khomeini — represents an intensification of the disruption to Iranian export infrastructure and reads through directly to the Sunday oil futures gap higher. Per data drop, Friday’s FCI Upstream cohort captured CVX ↑1.91% ($187.38) and OKE ↑0.56% ($93.52) among the top movers; the widening theater and further strikes overnight suggest that follow-through into Monday’s cash tape is likely if positioning does not preempt. Iranian Foreign Minister Araghchi Signals Openness to Negotiations — A Notable Rhetorical Shift Against a Backdrop of Continued Retaliation
Per Al Jazeera and Times of Israel coverage, Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi indicated over the weekend that Tehran would need to negotiate if a decisive military outcome is not achievable, though he framed talks as appropriate only from a position of Iranian strength.
